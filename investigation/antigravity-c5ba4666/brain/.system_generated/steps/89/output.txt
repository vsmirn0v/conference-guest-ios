    Mac: catch-up finds undated live text; times and reactions in live text
    
    Catch-up placed live text only by the provider's createdAt. Lines without
    it (or with a seconds or differently named timestamp) were never inside any
    sound-off period, so catch-up showed "No transcript" although live text had
    lines for that time.
    
    - Live chat and transcript events record when they arrived. A line is placed
      by the provider's time when it agrees with arrival (within 10 minutes),
      otherwise by arrival; history pages keep provider time only.
    - Provider times are read from createdAt, timestamp, startTime, startedAt or
      time, in seconds or milliseconds; pre-2020 values count as absent.
    - The first live transcript event logs its field names (no content).
    - Live text shows a time on each line and lists reactions among the lines;
      catch-up periods show line times and the reactions sent in them. Chat
      messages without a provider time sort by arrival.
