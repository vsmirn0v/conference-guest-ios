# CoreAudio duplex startup R6

This separate opt-in Mac candidate layers on frozen [R5 retirement](coreaudio-retirement-r5.md). It keeps the peer factory, peers, APM and ADB stable while retiring the prior output generation before first authorized input negotiation. Raw input discards data until full UID/ASBD observations and advancing callbacks establish an exact generation seal. Fresh capture/output converters are built once against that seal. Later route changes fail muted; there is no automatic reconnect, converter reset or retired-owner reopening.

Artifact: `.build/airpods-transition-startup/coreaudio-duplex-r6/LiveKitWebRTC.xcframework`. [Lock](coreaudio-duplex-r6.lock.json) and [source patch](coreaudio-duplex-r6.patch) bind the frozen R5 baseline. `experimentalCoreAudioDuplexStartup=true` enables the separate `ROCK_COREAUDIO_DUPLEX_R6` app compile condition when the CoreAudio experiment is explicitly requested. Ordinary factory construction and legacy paths remain separate. HAL preparation and retirement run on the caller control queue, with short ADB bookends on the WebRTC worker. Software channels remain48k mono capture/stereo render, independently of physical route rate/channels. Physical bandwidth or AEC quality cannot be inferred from app rate.

User intent, route/demand invalidation and final publication use exact integer authority tokens. A separate output authority preserves valid render through microphone mute changes. Mic owner/revision and track identity are rechecked after async server/native work before sender enable; Sound-only reconciliation obtains a fresh exact seal without manufacturing a new mic request. Capture remains gated until current server/user authorization commits.

Permanent callback tombstones and immutable inner capture generations prevent queued callbacks from accessing destroyed state. Native stop/destroy/drain/worker/disposal/listener proof archives remain available.
