# Rock’n’Roll app icon

The app uses two interlocking, rounded ribbons to suggest a record's movement and people connecting. Amber and coral provide contrast against a deep midnight background. There is no embedded text or provider branding.

The final asset is `RockNRoll/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`: opaque RGB PNG, 1024 × 1024, with square corners. Xcode supplies the device sizes; iOS applies its corner mask. The [Apple asset catalog guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/configuring-your-app-icon) documents this single-size workflow. System dark/tinted treatments use this base artwork; custom appearance variants are not supplied.

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The first concept was simplified for readability at home-screen sizes. The final generated image was resized from 1254 × 1254 using macOS `sips`; no creative changes were made during export.

Verified on 23 September 2026: signed device build, compiled iPhone and iPad icon files, icon registration in the built Info.plist, and installation/launch on iVitalii. The compiled 120 × 120 iPhone icon was inspected visually. The installed app reports the display name Rock’n’Roll. The project and target are named `RockNRoll`; the existing bundle identifier and link scheme retain compatibility with the installed prototype.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production iOS app icon, single square 1024 x 1024, opaque full-bleed artwork.
Create a modern, beautiful premium app icon for “Rock’n’Roll”, a native video conferencing app. The name is context only: no letters or words in the artwork.
Design a striking, simple abstract mark that combines the flowing circular rhythm of a vinyl record with two people connecting in a conversation. One bold, memorable silhouette, substantial rounded geometric forms, a generous clear center and balanced negative space. Subtle dimensional material and careful light, polished and tactile rather than busy or photorealistic. Refined vibrant warm foreground against a very deep ink background; choose a harmonious accent sparingly. Strong contrast and excellent legibility at tiny home-screen size. Center the mark with comfortable optical padding. Artwork reaches all four square edges.
Avoid text, monograms, musical notes, guitars, headphones, generic video camera glyphs, provider logos, tiny details, thin grooves, lens flares, rainbow gradients, noisy textures, mockup devices, presentation boards, borders, drop shadows outside the image, pre-rounded app corners, transparent pixels. Deliver only the final square icon artwork.

## Final refinement prompt

The initial generated concept was supplied as the edit reference.

Refine this into a much more minimal, premium iOS app icon for Rock’n’Roll. Retain the deep midnight background and warm amber-to-coral material, but replace the entire detailed emblem with just TWO substantial smooth interlocking curved ribbons. Together they form one dynamic circular abstract mark, like two broad offset record grooves meeting in conversation. A clean open negative-space center, confident silhouette, simple rounded geometry, subtle bevel and soft light only. No separate heads, no human bodies, no literal vinyl record, no grooves, no dots, no text, no outlines, no small details. Make the mark optically centered and occupy about 65 percent of canvas width, with generous clear background. Beautiful restraint, closer to an expertly designed contemporary brand symbol than an illustration. The complete image is an opaque square 1024x1024, full-bleed midnight background with square corners; iOS supplies the corner mask. Only final production icon artwork, no mockup.
