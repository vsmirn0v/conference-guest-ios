## Screen and copy

**Support the author** is searchable under “support”, “gift” and “donate”. Explain that Rock’n’Roll is free and open source, all features are available to everyone, and supporting the author is voluntary. Describe testing calls, fixing audio problems and maintaining macOS compatibility as the author’s ongoing work. Support does not purchase a custom feature, a promised update or priority service. Introduce all routes as **support**, then use their own specific names when the user chooses one.

Group **Source on GitHub**, **iOS public beta (TestFlight)** and **rock.glowsoft.ru** together below the introduction. Keep each link available offline for copying and distinguish the app website from technical help. The project’s first-party code is Apache-2.0 with a NOTICE; third-party components retain their own terms. The beta link takes the reader to Apple’s public invitation. Support collection remains exclusive to the direct Mac releases. ## One invitation per calendar month

Place an inline invitation below **Open meeting history**. Example copy is **“Finding Rock’n’Roll useful? You can support the author.”** The sole action is **Support the author**. Users can keep reading, navigate away, or join a call normally. Keep the row static for that Home visit and preserve keyboard focus and scroll position. When someone activates the invitation with the keyboard, move focus to the Support heading. When they navigate back to Home, move focus to the Home heading.

| Decision | Proposed rule |
| --- | --- |
| Regular use | Begin after five distinct local days with five or more connected meeting minutes within the last 60 days, and at least 14 days since the first qualifying day. Combine intervals across reconnects and count overlapping intervals once. Use locally observed meetings. |
| Presentation | Evaluate at the start of a deliberate foreground Home visit. Across all windows, defer during a call, including detached, held and reconnecting sessions, join setup, screen sharing, companion activity, text entry, sheets, or urgent recovery and update UI. Skip the automatic return from a meeting. A newly arriving join always wins. |
| Cadence | One visible impression per eligible local calendar month per Mac. Claim it across all windows only once the row enters the viewport, release any invisible reservation when the visit ends, and store the most recent shown year/month atomically. Redraws, relaunches, stable/beta updates or a clock going backward cannot replay it. A skipped month expires without accumulating reminders. |
| Donor status | Suppress only for verified active **monthly** support. One-time transfers, starting checkout and unverified bank standing orders leave the ordinary cadence in place. When verified support ends, the next eligible Home visit may show the invitation if that month has no impression yet. |
| Privacy | Keep the qualifying day buckets, first qualifying date and latest impression locally. Prune day buckets after 60 days. Exclude room IDs, names, donors and bank data from telemetry, diagnostics and iCloud library sync. The current design has one eligible distribution, direct Mac, with shared local state for its stable and beta builds. |

Use ordinary Home layout. No timer inserts the row while a person is working. The invitation needs no OS notification permission. Visiting Settings voluntarily always works. ### Linking a subscription from a phone

1. After subscribing on the phone, open **Settings → App → Support the author → Monthly** on the Mac and choose **Link this Mac**. Use the same entry point to restore an existing subscription on a new Mac.
2. The Mac displays a single-use code valid for 30 minutes. On the phone, sign in to the Boosty account that holds the subscription. Open the author’s profile directly or scan the profile QR on the Mac, then send the code in a private message to the author. The QR contains only the public profile URL. The donor can keep using the app while the author checks the subscription. [Author’s Boosty profile](https://boosty.to/rwrt)
3. The Mac shows **Awaiting verification**. Checking the status again keeps the claim pending until the account, tier and paid-through date have been verified. A used or expired code cannot link another Mac.
4. After verification, show **Monthly support active through [date]** on this Mac. Use a locally formatted, unambiguous calendar date. Only a current, confirmed billing period suppresses the monthly reminder.

The interactive draft simulates the code and verification result locally. Only an explicitly labeled preview control supplies a confirmed result. Boosty lets creators restrict who can send private messages by subscription level. Before launch, test messaging access at each of the four tiers, verify the paid-through date in the creator’s records and exercise a renewal. [Boosty’s private messaging settings](https://boosty.to/boosty/posts/ed018408-7711-4f2d-9534-8b1c01529b9b)

**Supporter recognition.** A hosted checkout can accept a subscription without any app integration. The current research established Boosty’s creator-facing subscriber table, filters and CSV export. Its Telegram integration can grant tier-linked group access, but group membership alone supplies neither an individual payment record nor a paid-through date. The signed-in creator account’s subscriber table was empty on 19 November 2026, so no paid subscriber record could be tested. The research did not establish a supported public OAuth scope, webhook, or external API giving Rock’n’Roll an authenticated donor-to-Mac link and the paid-through date. A direct URL, a QR scan, a checkout return and a one-time Hipolink or T-Bank receipt cannot issue a monthly supporter credential. Keep provider session cookies out of the app and backend. [Boosty’s subscriber table and exports](https://boosty.to/boosty/posts/790103a8-b6fa-437f-9a65-42f33449c4cc) · [Boosty’s Telegram integration](https://boosty.to/boosty/posts/9b7e8eab-2a04-4fd9-a261-7e33f995f0ab)

The proposed early release uses a manual check of the creator’s billing record. Keep bulk subscriber exports off the public site, out of app bundles and out of Git. An automated version depends on a provider-approved integration with equivalent evidence.

1. **Set up.** Open the hosted checkout only when the user chooses to continue on Boosty. Choosing **Link this Mac** starts a separate claim with an opaque, expiring, single-use code and a device keypair. Store the private key in this Mac’s Keychain. The server binds the claim to the public key and a random supporter record. A direct Boosty tier URL has no verified facility for attaching an app-specific checkout reference. Bank logins, card data, meeting identity and app usage stay outside this association.
2. **Verify.** Check the account, tier, successful contribution and covered period through the confirmed mechanism. For a future supported API, authenticate callbacks when available and reconcile missed, duplicate or out-of-order events against authoritative status. Issue a separately signed, device-bound status receipt with the recipient, monthly cadence, effective period, status revision and refresh deadline.
3. **Use.** Suppress the Home invitation throughout the period the provider has confirmed. Refresh an enrolled supporter’s receipt in the background around an eligible Home visit or when the support screen opens. The refresh deadline is a retry target. If the check fails, honor the signed receipt through its confirmed period end, bounded to one billing period. A verified earlier termination takes effect immediately. An offline Mac learns about an early termination at the next successful check; it cannot extend the receipt into a new month without proof of renewal. After coverage expires, check for renewal before showing that month’s invitation when connectivity permits.
4. **End or restore.** Use provider-reported effective dates for cancellation, unpaid renewals, pauses and reversals. An immediate cancellation ends suppression at the next verified check. A cancellation scheduled for period end honors the currently funded period through that date. Recover support on another Mac through **Link this Mac** and a fresh claim from the subscribed Boosty account, then issue a receipt for the new device. Never accept a screenshot, typed name or reusable public supporter code as proof. If the provider cannot support restoration, scope automatic recognition to the enrolled Mac and say so before setup.

The status endpoint only needs the opaque supporter record, provider reference, confirmed status revision and effective dates. Treat renewal and cancellation as distinct from the public destination catalog. Keep status-signing keys separate from both recipient and software-update keys. Restrict access to the private record, minimize logs and identifiers, and document retention and provider handoff before release.

## Crypto concept for legal review

Keep crypto outside the initial build’s allowed methods, public site and signed public data until separately cleared. The local review uses the author-supplied public receiving addresses. BTC, KAS, SOL, TRX, native ETH on Ethereum mainnet and native BNB on BNB Smart Chain have routable QRs. The Ethereum and BNB choices share an EVM address but the QR payloads include different chain IDs (1 and 56). Copy returns the displayed address. BTC and KAS pass their network checksums, the mixed-case EVM address passes EIP-55, TRX passes its network and checksum checks, and the Solana address decodes to 32 bytes. The QR assets were decoded locally. These checks establish encoding and agreement with the supplied values; the owner must separately verify control and wallet compatibility. [Exact addresses, QR payloads and file names](../design/author-support/destinations.json)

The author confirmed **USDT and USDC on Ethereum mainnet, ERC-20**. Both use the supplied EVM beneficiary. USDT uses the issuer-listed Ethereum token contract `0xdac17f958d2ee523a2206206994597c13d831ec7`; native USDC uses `0xA0b86991c6218b36c1d19D4a2e9Eb0cE3606eB48`. The two local QRs encode separate ERC-681 requests to each token’s transfer function on chain ID 1 with the supplied beneficiary. The donor chooses the amount in a compatible wallet. Show the full beneficiary address, selected token and chain beside the QR and keep **Copy receiving address** available for wallets that need manual entry. The contract identifies the asset and must never be copied as the beneficiary. The Tron and Solana choices represent their native coins. [Tether Ethereum token](https://tether.to/en/supported-protocols/) · [Circle Ethereum token](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses)

Russian Federal Law 282-FZ, Article 1(6), restricts accepting crypto as payment or consideration for goods, intellectual output, work and services. Article 1(9) also restricts disseminating information about such use. Article 1(11) addresses offers and invitations to offer acquisition by persons for whom a broker may not acquire crypto under Article 31. A genuinely gratuitous gift has a different factual basis from remuneration. The proposed screen and public mirror still need a legal review covering the author, intended audience, publication and each proposed asset. The words “personal gift” alone do not establish eligibility. [Official publication of 282-FZ](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202608040007) · [Text of Article 1](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_540983/27f9ddea0cccf9a6b90bb2cb8b545d436f18157b/)

Ask counsel two distinct questions. Can this named person receive a gratuitous transfer of this asset on this network? Can the app and its public metadata invite this audience to send it? Civil Code Article 572 describes a transfer free of consideration. Article 582 gives public-purpose donations their own rules. Put the author’s identity at the point of transfer and reserve **personal gift** for the gratuitous wallet transfer under review. Keep the periodic Home invitation generic. [Civil Code, Part Two, Chapter 32](https://rospatent.gov.ru/ru/documents/2-civil-code-of-the-russian-federation-part-two/download)

3. **Separate freshness from safety.** A remote envelope’s expiry limits acceptance of a newly downloaded update. 5. **Handle a forced retirement.** Prefer another approved bundled or cached rail and show its normal instructions. If every known rail has actually been withdrawn, keep the author’s explanation and source link, omit withdrawn actions, and offer the normal signed app update when one is known.
