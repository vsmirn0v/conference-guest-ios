# A single meeting history for RockNRoll on Mac

Design draft · 20 November 2026 · Russian interface copy

After a call, open **История встреч**, select the date, and find the summary, full available transcript, tasks and notes together. When you move from Mac to iPhone and back, that date should still have one meeting. **Задачи** opens your commitments across meetings. **В эфире** manages calls in progress. **Ассистент** opens directly into a conversation.

| **История встреч** | One dated row for each attended occurrence, including unscheduled meetings. Search by title, participant or available transcript text. Group by date. A series filter shows its dates individually. Opening a search hit selects its section and scrolls to the matched passage. |
| **Задачи** | The cross-meeting task list. Keep the existing mine/all toggle, open/completed filters, assignee search, grouping by meeting, archive, reminders and undo. | **Ассистент** | A normal visit opens **Разговор**, retaining the conversation draft. **Моменты** stays one tab away. A moment notification opens that specific moment. A meeting or task named in an answer links to its canonical record. |
| Home, calendar, favorites, recent rooms and settings | Retain quick join, event browsing, saved-room management and preferences. Calendar offers **Открыть встречу** on an attended occurrence. An unvisited event belongs on the calendar. Recent rooms remain reusable invitation shortcuts. |

Old **Рабочая область встреч → В эфире** and per-call sidebar or menu-bar links select the corresponding call inside **В эфире**. Workspace Review and the Home history link open **История встреч**. Preserve back navigation, selection, unsaved edits and scroll position when following a source link. A deleted target offers a way back to the list.

From a historical record, **Вернуться во встречу** opens prejoin with that occurrence selected and presents the microphone and camera state. Returning to a call the Mac is already in simply focuses it. If the call has ended, its History row can still offer the room invitation for a new call. In **Разговор**, distinguish asking a private question about a record from sending a message into an active meeting. Open a source in History without losing the assistant conversation. Keep saved assistant guidance and local-only reply history accessible from the relevant meeting or moment, respecting the journal's existing export rules. Selecting a call changes the controls on screen. A separate explicit action transfers microphone ownership. Keep the existing full call window, detached window, compact window, camera/share controls, parallel transcripts and pause/restore assistant modes within reach. Opening History during a call keeps media running. When the selected call ends, offer **Открыть встречу** for its record and continue to show any other active calls. ## Two layouts for the same record

**Список рядом** is the default Mac layout. Keep the app sidebar, a searchable meeting list and the selected meeting side by side. The list shows the date, title, actual time span and only those status details that change the next action, such as a pending transcript. The detail header shows the title and date, followed by **Сводка · Расшифровка · Задачи · Заметки**. **Участие и календарь** opens a sheet for the calendar link, attendance intervals, devices and sync status. This layout suits comparing several days or working through outstanding tasks.

**Режим чтения** expands the same meeting to a readable text column and puts the list behind a Back action. It suits reviewing a long transcript or editing notes. Switching layout preserves the selected meeting, tab, query and reading position. Use this layout automatically in a narrow window. Give the transcript and note editor their available width before compressing either. At larger text sizes, wrap labels or expose an accessible section menu. Keyboard users can move from list to detail, switch sections, find text and return to the originating row. VoiceOver reads dates, names, playback-independent timestamps, task state and sync status in words. Color supplements those labels.

The first draft shows the release summary just after the iPhone contribution arrives. The second shows the full transcript of the same sample, including the gap before the Mac reconnects. ## How returning to a meeting should work

Give an attended occurrence a durable meeting ID. Store its calendar series separately for browsing related dates. In calendar-backed meetings retain the portable external series identifier, original occurrence instant, current scheduled start/end and the last known event title. EventKit already supplies the series and original instance date. The schedule's existing sync key uses the current scheduled start, which can change when the event is moved. Persist the association at join time so history remains readable after the schedule moves, expires from the agenda or becomes inaccessible. [Calendar reader](../RockNRoll/AppleCalendarReader.swift#L67) · [Current schedule key](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/CalendarMeetings.swift#L175)

Store each join as its own interval with a device ID, stable interval ID, join/leave times and available capture evidence. Use a common credential-free room key derived from engine, normalized origin and room ID. Reuse verified endpoint aliases where supported. Store the invitation for joining separately. Display labels, login links and calendar titles are unsuitable identifiers. [Room identity](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/CalendarMeetings.swift#L11) · [Existing per-device interval](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/MeetingSync/MeetingSyncModels.swift#L49)

| Evidence at join or later sync | Proposed action |
| --- | --- |
| Same durable meeting ID from a reconnect, explicit handoff or supported continuation | Attach the interval on either device. Keep its original device, timestamps and capture references. Replay of the same interval is idempotent. |
| Same verified calendar occurrence and compatible room, with no conflicting event or prior split | Attach automatically for the scheduled window and up to **30 minutes after scheduled end**. An already connected participant can continue past that threshold. The grace period concerns a new join. |
| Another scheduled occurrence starts in the same room | End the implicit rejoin allowance at that event's start. Respect an explicit calendar selection. For ambiguous raw-room joins or an ongoing overrun, show both dated titles and times. Keep the existing separate-session or continuous-chapter preference when explicitly chosen. [Current transitions](../NativeMacGuest/DesktopWorkspace.swift#L619) |
| Room-only join, unavailable calendar, changed invitation or conflicting calendars | Start a usable provisional record. Suggest a link when room, times and other evidence agree. Show both timelines and data to be combined. **Объединить** confirms, **Оставить отдельно** suppresses the suggestion. A later sync can supply stronger evidence. |
| Opening a historical occurrence after its window | Offer to join the room as a new meeting. Keep any correction to the historical occurrence explicit in **Участие и календарь**. A series or repeated title alone never combines two dates. |

When a user links, splits or undoes a merge, persist the decision on both devices. Keep original record IDs as routable aliases and retain original event, note, task and summary provenance, so a split can restore ownership. When a manually added note or task has no identifiable original interval, ask where it belongs during the split. Later imports honor this decision. A series with a changed room, two events with the same title, or a recurring event rescheduled across time zones can then be corrected without rewriting neighboring dates.

## Sync, late arrivals and summary updates

Show local changes immediately. With workspace sync enabled on both devices in the same iCloud account, request updates after joins and edits, on foreground or network recovery, and when the active History view receives remote changes. Retry transient failures with backoff and provide **Синхронизировать**. Drive indicators from the individual record and content type. Useful states include **Только на этом устройстве**, **Ожидаем данные с iPhone**, **Синхронизировано** and **Повторить** with the failed step and last successful time. Show a waiting device only when its participation is known.

The current Mac controls are in **Настройки → Основные → На всех ваших устройствах**. The iCloud switch, **Синхронизировать историю встреч, итоги, заметки и задачи**, and the raw transcript retention policy answer different questions. In the new History, a record missing text should link straight to the relevant control. Show **Ожидаем данные с iPhone** only for a known pending contribution that the current policy permits. If the phone has never advertised any such material, show the last confirmed receipt and offer a refresh. Offer a local archive as a source when one actually exists. [Mac settings entry](../NativeMacGuest/DesktopHomeView.swift#L719) · [Transcript preferences](../RockNRoll/RoomSyncSettingsView.swift#L42) · [Private cloud database](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/MeetingSync/CloudEnvelopeTransport.swift#L214)

A summary, task or note can arrive independently of raw text. Respect the separate raw-text policy, which currently supports off, 7, 14, 30 and 90 days. A local archived transcript can remain readable after the cloud window. If a device has no copy, label only what the app can establish, such as **Срок хранения в iCloud истёк** or **Расшифровка не получена**. Offer the appropriate source device, settings or retry action. [Retention model](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/MeetingSync/MeetingSyncModels.swift#L4) · [Local transcript archive](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/MeetingSync/TranscriptArchiveStore.swift)

Build each summary against a revision of the deduplicated transcript and eligible inputs. Reuse the shared transcript merger and preserve source bookmarks, speaker attribution and gaps. Track known participation separately from confirmed capture. Count overlapping attendance once. When later text adds evidence, show **Доступна новая часть расшифровки** and **Обновить сводку**. With automatic generation enabled, one elected writer may prepare a new draft when the meeting settles. Keep the previous version readable while it runs. Replaying identical chunks or retrying a failed request for the same revision must produce one accepted version. Current Mac retry uses saved request input. Current iPhone generation rereads the transcript and replaces the local task list, so the new revision flow needs explicit reconciliation. [Transcript merge](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/MeetingSync/TranscriptMergeEngine.swift) · [Mac retry](../NativeMacGuest/Attention/AttentionCoordinator.swift#L529) · [iPhone generation and save](../RockNRoll/Workspace/IOSWorkspaceCoordinator.swift#L490)

Show the added passages and a comparison for sections edited by the user. Applying an update keeps notes, title overrides, accepted task IDs, dates, reminders, owners and completion states. New task candidates require acceptance. Where explicit evidence identifies an existing task, link the new citation. A partial or expired transcript remains eligible for a summary with a coverage label if the configured writer has sufficient input. Existing outcome merge already prioritizes human edits and supports review candidates. [Outcome merge](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/MeetingSync/OutcomeAndTaskMergeEngine.swift#L58) · [Task reconciliation](../ConferenceCore/Sources/ConferenceCore/MeetingSync/OutcomeAndTaskMergeEngine.swift#L159)

## Delivering it in stages

1. Route existing Mac views into the new sidebar and meeting reader. Union available journal/library records, including journal-only, summary-only, local-only and imported records. Keep one canonical task query. Redirect old links and preserve draft state.
2. Add versioned occurrence links, per-join intervals, canonical room keys and bidirectional handoff IDs to the shared schema. Treat missing fields from older clients as unknown. For old records, link on strong evidence, stage ambiguous matches for review and preserve original IDs, edits, export receipts and deletion markers. Make migration repeatable.
3. Add per-record delivery state, automatic refresh, meeting coverage and versioned summary generation from merged evidence. Test with separate real devices, the shipping iCloud environment and a mixture of old and new records.

## Acceptance walkthrough

- **Friday, 20 November, Сверка релиза.** Calendar schedules 10:00–10:30. Join on Mac 10:00–10:18, on iPhone 10:16–10:27, then on Mac 10:29–10:42. Both devices should eventually show one meeting with three intervals. Show **40 minutes of participation**, the two-minute overlap, the 10:27–10:29 gap and a 12-minute overrun. Transcript coverage is reported from captured material. Select a citation from the iPhone interval on Mac and read the source line in context.
- **Adjacent events.** Add a distinct event at 10:30 in the same room. A direct room rejoin at 10:34 offers a dated choice. Selecting the second event attaches the new interval there. Undo restores the original membership. Selecting **Оставить отдельно** survives sync and relaunch. Next Friday's recurrence opens its own record.
- **Delayed iPhone text.** Finish the call with iPhone offline. Edit the Mac summary, complete a task and add a note. When iPhone returns, its passages appear once. Compare the new draft, keep the edited text and check the existing task and note in both views. With raw sync off, a received summary still opens with an accurate transcript availability state.
- **Navigation and continuity.** Launch Assistant into **Разговор** and follow a moment notification into **Моменты**. Follow a task to its source and back to the same task filter. With two calls running, switch the displayed call, open History and return. Verify microphone ownership, mute and sharing controls still target the intended call.
- **Legacy and accessibility.** Open a transcript-only journal, an imported summary and a pre-migration reminder. Follow their IDs through an accepted merge and an undo. Repeat a choice using only the keyboard and VoiceOver in a narrow window. Compare the two layouts on the same transcript passage and verify that focus, selection and reading position survive.
