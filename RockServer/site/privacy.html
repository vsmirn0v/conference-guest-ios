PRIVACY

Clear by design.

Rock’n’Roll lets you join live group conversations. This page explains the information needed to make that work. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Accounts and media

You can join without an account. The name you enter is shown to other people in the jam. The app saves that name on your device for future joins. The app does not access your address book. Your microphone and camera start off; you choose when to publish their live streams. On supported desktop browsers, you may also choose to share your screen; that live stream is visible to people in the jam until you stop it. Rock’n’Roll does not operate a recording service for rooms at this domain and does not save their audio or video. Other participants may use their own recording tools, so share only what you are comfortable sharing.

Which service handles a room?

Our public demo runs on our own LiveKit server. Other invitation links connect to the service chosen by the organizer, such as a compatible self-hosted LiveKit room, SaluteJazz, Yandex Telemost or TrueConf. That service handles admission, your display name, connection details and any media or messages you publish under its own policies. The organizer may enable recording or server transcripts. Rock’n’Roll does not use advertising or behavioral analytics.

Saved rooms and meeting history

The app saves up to ten recent rooms and any rooms you star. Remove invitations from your room list at any time. Our demo server delivers chat live. The app’s meeting history can retain chat, available transcript text, speaker names, meeting details, notes, summaries and tasks after you leave. You can delete a meeting from History. A separate Catch up log marks audio interruptions and expires after 24 hours or when you leave.

Transcript archives can also be kept on your device, backed up to a folder you choose or exported. A folder managed by another storage provider follows that provider’s sync and retention settings. Manage your exported copies in that folder.

Speech recognition and generated voices

When transcription is active, the selected recognizer processes meeting audio. Apple Speech uses on-device recognition where the device and language support it. Otherwise Apple may process audio on its servers. The optional downloaded Russian speech model runs locally. If it is unavailable, the app can use Apple Speech. You can turn off transcription and manage Speech Recognition permission in system settings.

System speech and an authorized Apple Personal Voice generate audio from your typed reply on the device. On supported hardware you can download an optional neural voice model, record or import a sample of your own voice, and delete the saved voice features in settings. Model downloads contact Hugging Face. If you choose a Rapid-MLX voice server, it receives your reply text, voice instructions and model selection. Sending a spoken reply shares the generated audio with the meeting service and participants.

Meeting Assistant and AI providers

Meeting Assistant can use available chat and transcripts to flag mentions, suggest replies and help produce summaries, decisions and tasks. These features can use an available on-device model. If you configure OpenAI, OpenRouter or a custom AI endpoint, requests send meeting content relevant to the feature to that provider. This can include speaker names, excerpts from transcripts and chat, the meeting title, your notes and your instructions. Your provider handles requests under its own policies. The app stores your API key in Keychain. Check the selected provider and automatic summary and reply settings before using these features with others.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off until you enable it. It uses your private iCloud database to share your saved name, starred rooms, custom titles and, if included, recent invitations. A complete invitation can contain a password. On iPhone and iPad, workspace sync and calendar schedule sharing are initially selected when you turn on iCloud sync. Depending on your settings, synced data can also include meeting history, notes, summaries, tasks, settings, calendar meeting details and recent transcript or chat entries. Raw transcript sync initially covers 30 days. You can turn it off or change the period under Across your devices. Local history and archive files have separate lifetimes.

Turning sync off keeps local copies. Use Delete synced data to request removal of shared data, and manage local history and exported files separately. Sync pauses if your Apple Account changes. Apple’s iCloud privacy and storage settings apply.

Optional meeting continuation

After enabling iCloud sync, you can separately enable active-jam sharing on each device. A short-lived advertisement in your private iCloud database includes the complete invitation, room title, name used in that jam, a random device and session identifier, a generic device type and the last update time. This lets you continue a jam on another device using the same Apple Account. Coordination records track the move; no live audio, video, chat or transcript is transferred through iCloud. The original device briefly pauses audio and leaves only after the new device connects. Microphone and camera start off on the new device. Active status expires if updates stop, and expired invitation and name content is removed when a participating device next refreshes. Disabling active-jam sharing withdraws this device’s advertisement when iCloud is reachable; an offline advertisement expires. Delete synced data also removes these advertisements and move records. Apple Handoff discovery uses only opaque device and session identifiers, without invitation passwords.

Optional Calendar meetings

Calendar access is off by default. If you enable it, the app reads event titles, times and invitation links from calendars you select to suggest meetings and associate rooms with scheduled events. The app never edits your calendar. Calendar meeting details may be shared with your other devices if iCloud sync and Share calendar schedule are on. Disabling calendar access clears derived event information on that device. When you explicitly star a calendar room, its saved name and invitation follow your existing iCloud Sync preference. To identify a meeting engine, the app can make bounded HTTPS requests to the invitation website’s discovery or room-metadata API. These probes do not send calendar titles, your display name or invitation passwords, and do not join a meeting. Optional automatic joining has a cancellable countdown and keeps microphone and camera off.

Mac app updates

The Mac app can request our public update feed and download a new app version. These requests process the network address and technical request information needed to deliver the update. Your chosen stable or beta channel stays on your Mac. Update checks do not send meeting invitations, display names, calendar contents, audio, video or chat to our update service. We do not use update checks for advertising or behavioral analytics.

Optional support in the direct Mac app

The Mac keeps its use-day count and the month of its last support reminder locally. Opening the support screen checks the published donation details on rock.glowsoft.ru and GitHub. A payment opens the selected provider, which handles billing under its own terms.

If you choose to link a monthly Boosty subscription, your Mac sends a random device-key fingerprint and a random claim reference to rock.glowsoft.ru. You send the resulting one-time code to the author privately from your subscribed Boosty account. The author checks that account and its paid-through date. A signed record lets your Mac check its status and skip reminders during the confirmed period. Unused codes expire after 30 minutes. The service keeps issued records for renewal and revocation. Meeting names, participants, usage history and card details are excluded from these requests. You can contact the author below about an enrollment.

Website and service records

Cloudflare provides DNS for this domain. Website and room traffic normally connect directly to our server, which processes network addresses and technical request information to deliver the service. We minimize retained logs and do not put room access tokens in application logs. Requests to another meeting service may be processed and retained by its operator. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices and contact

You can leave a room, keep your microphone and camera off, turn off transcription, delete local meeting history and manage sync or archive copies. For a privacy question, correction, or report, email devcore@gmail.com. We will update this page if our data practices change.
