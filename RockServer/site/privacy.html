PRIVACY

Clear by design.

Rock’n’Roll lets you join live group conversations. This page explains the information needed to make that work. Last updated 24 September 2026.

Accounts and media

You can join without an account. The name you enter is shown to other people in the jam. The app saves that name on your phone for future joins; choosing a name from Contacts copies only the selected name. Your microphone and camera start off; you choose when to publish their live streams. Rock’n’Roll does not operate a recording service for rooms at this domain and does not save their audio or video. Other participants may use their own recording tools, so share only what you are comfortable sharing.

Which service handles a room?

Links on rock.glowsoft.ru use our self-hosted real-time room service. The app also accepts compatible guest invitations from other meeting services. A room opened from another service is handled under that service’s own policies and controls; its organizer may enable features such as transcripts or recording. The app obtains connection details from the selected service and a short-lived credential for rooms at this domain. We do not use advertising or behavioral analytics in our own app or website.

On your phone

The app keeps a protected local list of recent jam links (up to ten unstarred, plus starred links) so you can rejoin. You can remove any link from this list. Chat messages in our demo room are delivered live to participants and are not retained by our server. The app also keeps chat only for the current call. The app can keep a protected local Catch up history of times you may have missed meeting audio and transcript lines a room service actually supplies. This file stays on your phone, is excluded from backups, and can be deleted from the app. Leaving a meeting deletes that meeting’s local history; history older than 24 hours is discarded on the next launch. The app does not store your live audio or video in this file.

Website and service records

Cloudflare provides DNS for this domain. Website and room traffic normally connect directly to our server, which processes network addresses and technical request information to deliver the service. We minimize retained logs and do not put room access tokens in application logs. Requests to another meeting service may be processed and retained by its operator. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices and contact

You can leave a jam, keep your microphone and camera off, and delete local Catch up history. For a privacy question, correction, or report, email devcore@gmail.com. We will update this page if our data practices change.
