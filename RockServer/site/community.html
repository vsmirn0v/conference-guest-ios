Meeting etiquette.

These guidelines apply to conversations, presentations and music sessions in our public demo room.

In a meeting

Respect the other participants. Do not harass, threaten, share private information, or play material you lack permission to share. Ask before recording, transcribing or sharing someone’s contributions with an AI service. Use only your own voice or one you have permission to use when generating speech. Mute when you are not speaking or playing, and leave if the room is not for you.

Report a concern

Leave the room and email devcore@gmail.com with the time and room name. Please avoid sending private invitations or recordings unless we specifically request them. We can remove access to rooms we operate; other meeting services control their own rooms.
