Good music,

Rock’n’Roll is for respectful small-group music sessions. The test jam is public and intended for testing.

In a jam

Respect other musicians. Do not harass, threaten, share private information, or play material you lack permission to share. Ask before recording anyone. Mute when you are not speaking or playing, and leave if the room is not for you.

Report a concern

Leave the room and email devcore@gmail.com with the time and room name. Please avoid sending private invitations or recordings unless we specifically request them. We can remove access to rooms we operate; other meeting services control their own rooms.
