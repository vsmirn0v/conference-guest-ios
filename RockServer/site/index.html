Join a
meeting.

Open a link from your team, class or band. Rock’n’Roll brings meetings from different services into one app. Follow a screen share, read the conversation as it happens or type a reply for the room to hear.

Try the public demo ↗ Which links work? ↓

Bring your invitation.

On iPhone or iPad, paste the organizer’s link in Invitation link, enter your display name and tap Join. The app recognizes compatible LiveKit rooms, SaluteJazz guest meetings, Yandex Telemost calls and TrueConf web conferences that allow guest access.

For LiveKit, the host must offer a Rock’n’Roll-compatible HTTPS invitation and issue a room access token. Our public demo uses this same setup. Guest admission, chat and screen sharing depend on the host.

In the iPhone and iPad app

Switch between rooms

Keep up to four meetings open. Listen to another room while you work, adjust its volume and choose which meeting hears your microphone.

See what’s being shared

Watch the gallery or focus on a shared screen. Keep a presentation in Picture in Picture while taking notes, or share your own screen when the room allows it.

Follow live speech

Read a transcript supplied by the room, or turn on speech recognition for English or Russian. On-device recognition is available on supported devices and languages.

Speak a typed reply

Type a sentence and choose a room. Voice Reply generates speech and sends it into a supported call while your microphone stays muted. Use a system voice or your authorized Personal Voice.

Catch a question

Set Meeting Assistant to flag mentions of your name or follow a discussion for questions that need you. With a supported AI provider, it can suggest a chat or spoken reply for you to review.

Find the follow-up

Keep notes beside your meeting history. Generate editable summaries, decisions and tasks with a configured AI provider. Use calendar reminders to find your next call and private iCloud sync to take your work to another device.

Get Rock’n’Roll.

Try the current iPhone and iPad beta through TestFlight. On an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later, open the downloaded disk image and drag the app to Applications.
