Stay in the room.

To try the public demo, open the test room with a friend. You can join it from the iPhone or iPad app or a supported browser.

Join a meeting by invitation

On Home, paste the full HTTPS invitation, enter your name and tap Join. The same field accepts compatible LiveKit, Yandex Telemost and TrueConf invitations, as well as guest links from compatible enterprise meeting sites. Your organizer controls entry to their room. For guest app links that omit the host, choose the meeting website when prompted.

Host a compatible LiveKit room

Give guests an HTTPS invitation ending in /jams/ROOM_ID. On the same host, serve the room metadata with engine set to livekit and join_protocol set to rocknroll-v1. The join endpoint must issue a participant token and a secure WebSocket URL on that host. The public demo server implements both endpoints.

Having trouble?

Check that your link is complete, your network is available, and the other person is in the same room. Your microphone and camera begin off. If iOS interrupts audio for another call, return to the jam after the call ends.
